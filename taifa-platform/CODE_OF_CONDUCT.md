# Code of Conduct

Taifa contributors uphold professionalism, inclusion, and safety.

---

## Our standards

- Respectful communication in English and Swahili contexts  
- No harassment, discrimination, or retaliation  
- Constructive code and design review  
- Compliance with national law and organizational policies  

---

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **conduct@taifa.go.tz** (placeholder) or your engineering manager. Maintainers may remove contributors who violate these standards.
