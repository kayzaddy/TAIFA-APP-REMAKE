# Product Review Board (PRB)

**Mandate:** Product portfolio alignment, TPOS gate readiness, and go/no-go for Beta/GA.

---

## Scope

- `products/*` charters and roadmaps
- Feature scope vs platform capability
- Partner-facing product commitments

---

## Inputs

- TPOS gate evidence (00–25 pack per product)
- Portfolio roadmap deltas
