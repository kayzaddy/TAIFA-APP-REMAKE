# Engineering Review Board (ERB)

**Mandate:** Engineering excellence, Definition of Done, test strategy, and technical debt prioritization.

---

## Scope

- Engineering standards exceptions
- Monorepo tooling (`automation/`, `testing/`)
- Performance budgets and SLO proposals
- Cross-squad refactors

---

## Relationship to ARB

ERB does **not** override platform boundaries—escalate boundary questions to ARB.
