# Taifa Express — Rider Guide

## Role

Riders are Taifa Mobility drivers on `delivery_bike` trips created by Express after payment succeeds.

## What the rider receives

- Pickup (merchant) and dropoff (customer)  
- Navigation via Mobility  
- Estimated earnings from trip quote  
- Proof of delivery + optional SoftPOS / Tap via MAP  

## SoftPOS

Rider phones remain Smart POS through **Tap & Pay / MAP** — not a separate Express payments stack.

## Cash

Optional cash collection remains an ops policy; ledger settlement still goes through Taifa Payments when digital rails are used.
