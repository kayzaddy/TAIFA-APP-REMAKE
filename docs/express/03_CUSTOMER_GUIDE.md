# Taifa Express — Customer Guide

## Promise

Search, speak, or upload a list. Express finds the best nearby merchant, charges your Taifa Wallet, and assigns a rider.

## Ways to shop

1. **Search** — Milk, bread, soap…  
2. **Categories** — Groceries, pharmacy, vegetables…  
3. **AI assistant** — “Need breakfast.” / “Weekly groceries.”  
4. **Express checkout** — one tap after basket review  

## What you never choose (by default)

- Which shop wins  
- Which rider is assigned  
- Which settlement engine runs (always Taifa Payments)

## Payment

Wallet-first via Commerce food-order pay path. Tap-to-Pay on delivery can attach later through MAP Tap sessions (`tap_session_code` on the order).

## AI rule

AI **suggests** baskets. You review and pay. AI never authorizes payments.
