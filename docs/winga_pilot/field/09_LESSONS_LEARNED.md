# 9. Lessons Learned Report — Field (Week 0)

**Status:** Pre-launch · lessons limited to ops design, not marketplace outcomes.

---

## What we know (evidence-based)

1. **Software path works in lab** — pay and settle commission tested; AI cannot authorize payment.  
2. **Single-vertical focus is correct** — Hotels / Dar CBD reduces noise.  
3. **Scaffold ≠ onboarded** — 10 hotel / 20 Winga roster slots are recruitment tools, not proof of liquidity.  
4. **Organic acquisition is mandatory for validation** — paid ads would confound product-market fit.

---

## What we do not know yet

- Whether customers will pay for hotel stays via Winga without assistance  
- Whether Wingas can earn sustainable commissions  
- Whether hotels will retain after paying commission  
- Real CSAT, repeat rate, support load under volume
