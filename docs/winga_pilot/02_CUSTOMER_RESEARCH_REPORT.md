# 2. Customer Research Report — Hotels Pilot

**Status:** Protocol ready · Field interviews **0 / 25 target**  
**Do not invent satisfaction scores.** Lab findings below are product observations only.

---

## Research questions

1. Can customers find a suitable hotel stay without training?  
2. Do they trust paying through Taifa?  
3. What causes drop-off before pay?  
4. Would they book again via the same Winga?

---

## Method

| Method | n (target) | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guided first booking (moderated) | 8 | Week 1 |
| Unmoderated task completion | 15 | Weeks 2–4 |
| Exit interview (15–20 min) | 20 | After first paid stay |
| CSAT (1–5) post-fulfillment | All paid deals | Continuous |
| NPS | Week 6 | Cohort |

### Interview script (customers)

1. What were you trying to achieve today?  
2. How did you find the hotel / Winga?  
3. What made you trust (or doubt) the offer?  
4. Walk me through payment — anything unclear?  
5. What would make you book again?  
6. What almost made you quit?

Prioritize by **business impact** (blocks pay / trust), not request volume.
