# 6. Customer Success Playbook

**Owner:** Head of Customer Success / CCO  
**Support partner:** Principal Customer Support Lead  

---

## Mission

Customers complete trusted bookings and return.

---

## Support procedures (measurable)

| Case type | First response target | Resolution target | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account creation | ≤ 4h | ≤ 1 business day | Support |
| Booking assistance | ≤ 2h | Same day | Support + Winga Success |
| Quote assistance | ≤ 2h | Same day | Support + Winga |
| Payment support | ≤ 1h (Critical if funds) | Same day / Settlement | Support + Settlement |
| Refunds | ≤ 4h | Per policy + Finance | Settlement + Support |
| Complaints | ≤ 4h | ≤ 2 business days | CS |
| Disputes | ≤ 2h | Risk timeline | Risk + Settlement |
| Reviews | ≤ 1 business day | Coaching to Winga/Hotel | CS |
| Escalations | Immediate for Sev-1 | Per incident handbook | On-call |
| Retention outreach | Weekly list | Logged outcomes | CS |

Every ticket: category, severity, timestamps, CSAT (optional post-close).

---

## Customer Success program KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Time to first booking | Signup/recruit → first paid stay |
| Repeat booking rate | ≥2 paid stays / customers with ≥1 |
| CSAT | 1–5 post-stay / post-ticket |
| Retention | Active in last 60 days |
| NPS | Periodic survey |
| Referral rate | Bookings with referral source |

---

## Proactive plays

1. Day-1: confirm first quote received  
2. After pay: confirmation SMS/email check  
3. Post-stay: CSAT + review prompt  
4. Silent 30 days after stay: win-back (organic)  

---

## Research

Weekly interviews when volume exists. **n=0 → write n=0.** Never invent quotes. See field research rules in governance.
