# 5. Provider Success Playbook

**Owner:** Head of Provider Success  
**Cadence:** Continuous + Monthly Business Review (MBR)

---

## Mission

Providers stay because **lead quality and revenue** justify commission cost.

---

## Ongoing monitoring

| Signal | Review |
| --- | --- |
| Lead quality | Weekly sample (good vs spam) |
| Booking quality | Cancellations, no-shows, disputes |
| Campaign performance | Peak rules ROI |
| Revenue / GMV | Paid deals attributed |
| Commission cost | Settled commission vs GMV |
| Support requests | Ticket themes |
| Growth opportunities | Upsell packages / seasons |
| Retention intent | Quarterly pulse |

---

## Monthly Business Review agenda (60 min)

1. KPI scorecard (leads → quotes → paid → stays)  
2. Lead quality examples (2 good / 2 bad)  
3. Commission cost vs occupancy gained  
4. Support & disputes  
5. Campaign / inventory recommendations  
6. Commitments (owner + due date)  
7. Continue / at-risk / churn risk flag  

---

## At-risk triggers

- Response SLA missed 3× in a week  
- Zero paid bookings in 30 days while receiving leads  
- ≥2 open disputes  
- Explicit “considering exit”

**Action:** 48h outreach + recovery plan; escalate to Marketplace Ops if unresolved 7 days.

---

## Success manager model

Each live hotel has a named Provider Success owner. Coverage ratio target: ≤15 live hotels per manager at pilot scale; reassess at growth.
