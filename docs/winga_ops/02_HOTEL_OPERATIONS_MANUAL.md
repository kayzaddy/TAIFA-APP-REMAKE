# 2. Hotel Operations Manual

**Owner:** Head of Provider Success  
**Audience:** Hotel ops contacts · Taifa Provider Success  

---

## Role of the hotel on Winga

Hotels supply inventory, rates, and fulfillment. Wingas originate customers. Taifa settles payment and commission via the ledger.

Hotels do **not** redesign platform flows. Hotels follow this manual.

---

## Daily hotel responsibilities

1. Respond to Winga/customer inquiries within **SLA ≤ 4 business hours**.  
2. Confirm availability before quote acceptance.  
3. Confirm booking after payment (reference `payment_ref`).  
4. Honor cancellation policy disclosed at quote.  
5. Flag no-shows / disputes within 24h.  
6. Keep rates and inventory accurate.

---

## Booking lifecycle (hotel view)

Inquiry → Quote support → Acceptance → **Payment (Taifa)** → Hotel confirmation → Stay → Review → Settlement (Taifa pays Winga commission per agreement)

Hotel never pays Wingas directly outside Taifa settlement.

---

## Quality standards

| Standard | Bar |
| --- | --- |
| Lead response | ≤ 4 business hours |
| Confirmation after pay | ≤ 2 hours (business) |
| Rate integrity | Quote matches stay charge |
| Review response | ≤ 48 hours for negative reviews |
| Data accuracy | Inventory/pricing updated weekly minimum |

---

## Escalation

| Issue | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Guest complaint | Hotel ops + Customer Support |
| Payment not visible | Settlement Ops |
| Fraud suspicion | Risk & Trust |
| Platform outage | Eng Support via Marketplace Ops |
