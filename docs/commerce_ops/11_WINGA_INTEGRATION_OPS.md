# 11. Winga Integration Operations Guide

**Owner:** Merchant Success (A) · Winga Success (partner) · Settlement  

---

## Operationalize

1. **Publish offerings** — MOS product → `publish-winga`  
2. **Commission rules** — configured in Winga (no engine redesign)  
3. **Campaigns** — peak/referral codes documented  
4. **Lead monitoring** — Winga desk + merchant notifications  
5. **Commission verification** — match Winga events to paid deals  
6. **Settlement** — Winga settle-commission SOP  
7. **Performance review** — monthly: GMV via Winga vs direct  

## Merchant checklist before campaigns

- [ ] Operationally live (onboarding complete)  
- [ ] Inventory accurate enough for promised SKUs  
- [ ] Commission agreement signed  
- [ ] Sample publish tested  

## Forbidden

- Paying Wingas outside Taifa ledger  
- Publishing out-of-stock as available without policy
