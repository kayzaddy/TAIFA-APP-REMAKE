# 10. Staff Operations Guide

**Owner:** Store Ops + Merchant Success  

---

## Lifecycle

Role assignment → Shift schedule → Attendance → Training → Certification → Permissions → Performance review  

### Roles (MOS)
Owner · Manager · Cashier · Warehouse · Purchaser · Analyst · Support  

### POS permissions
- Cashier: sell, open/close own session  
- Manager: voids, discounts above threshold, close any session  

### Warehouse permissions
- Receive, count, fulfill  
- Large adjust: manager approval  

### Training
- Platform walkthrough (`/commerce` role app)  
- Money awareness: ledger only · AI never pays  
- SOP attestation  

### Performance
- Cashiers: sales/hour, void rate  
- Warehouse: fulfill SLA hit %  
- Managers: recon completion, exception aging  

### Reviews
Monthly light pulse · Quarterly formal for managers
