# 12 — MVP Definition

---

## Executive summary

**MVP:** A Tanzanian SME can **complete KYB**, **accept QR payments**, **see transactions**, **issue a refund**, and **receive payment notifications**—on web or mobile—within **2 weeks of approval**.

---

## In scope

- Identity login (owner + 1 cashier role)  
- TNPI merchant onboarding (sole prop + SME tier)  
- 1 branch, 2 employees, 1 device  
- Dynamic QR payment  
- Transaction list (7 days)  
- Full/partial refund (manager)  
- Digital receipt share  
- Dashboard: today sales, count, success rate  
- Push notification on payment  
- Audit log on refund  

---

## Out of scope (MVP)

- SoftPOS (TM-S7)  
- Payment links  
- Advanced analytics / AI (beta post-MVP)  
- Multi-branch reporting rollup  
- Customer CRM  
- Settlement payout initiation (view only optional)  

---

## Success criteria

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Onboarding → first payment | < 48h median |
| Payment success rate | ≥ 95% |
| App crash-free | ≥ 99% |
| TNPI boundary violations | 0 in CI scan |

---

## Launch geography

Dar es Salaam pilot; Swahili + English UI.
