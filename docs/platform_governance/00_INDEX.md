# Taifa Platform Governance Framework — Index

**Status:** Official constitutional standard (v1.0)  
**Scope:** Every current and future Taifa platform  
**Constraint:** Unified lifecycle — do not redesign product architectures; do not invent parallel governance systems  

This framework is the **governing system** for building, validating, certifying, launching, operating, and evolving every Taifa platform.

---

## Core principles

Every platform must be: **secure · observable · testable · operable · measurable · governable · reusable · certifiable**.

No platform is complete until it passes every required lifecycle stage with **evidence**.

---

## Lifecycle (mandatory)

```
0 Vision → 1 Architecture → 2 Engineering → 3 Experience → 4 Operations
→ 5 Pilot Readiness → 6 Business Validation → 7 Certification
→ 8 Production Launch → 9 Continuous Improvement
```

**No stage skip. No gate skip.**
