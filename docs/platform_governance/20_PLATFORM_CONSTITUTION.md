# 20. Taifa Platform Constitution

**Status:** Constitutional standard for the Taifa Digital Ecosystem  
**Authority:** Executive Steering Committee  

---

## Article I — Unity

There is **one** platform lifecycle, **one** certification system, and **one** money truth (the payments ledger). Domain platforms reuse shared services; they do not fork governance.

## Article II — Completeness

A platform is not complete until it passes Stages 0–8 with evidence. Continuous Improvement (Stage 9) never ends.

## Article III — Gates

No stage or gate may be skipped. Approvals are recorded with artifacts.

## Article IV — Security & Trust

Every platform shall be secure, observable, and auditable. Threat models precede build. Secrets never live in source control.

## Article V — Money

Financial events are ledger-backed. Domains store references, not balances. **AI shall not authorize payments.**

## Article VI — Operations

No launch without operable SOPs, monitoring, and incident response. Operations create trust.

## Article VII — Evidence

Certification is evidence-based. Metrics and interviews shall not be fabricated. Absence of data is reported as such.

## Article VIII — Reuse

Identity, Payments, Wallet, Ledger, Notifications, AI, Analytics, Governance, and Mobility are shared. Duplication of these capabilities is prohibited without Architecture Board exception.

## Article IX — Evolution

Change is governed: ADR for architecture, CIP for improvement, recertification on material risk.

## Article X — Supremacy

This Constitution, with the Governance Charter and Lifecycle Framework, is the supreme operating standard for Taifa platforms. Domain handbooks implement; they do not contradict.
