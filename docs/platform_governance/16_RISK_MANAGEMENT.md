# 16. Risk Management Framework

**Owner:** CRO · partners CISO, COO, Finance  

---

## Risk classes

Strategic · Operational · Financial · Security · Compliance · Technology · Third-party · AI  

## Process

Identify → Assess (impact × likelihood) → Treat (mitigate/transfer/accept/avoid) → Monitor → Report  

## Money-platform Critical risks (examples)

| Risk | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger / capture mismatch | Freeze + recon SOP |
| Double pay | Idempotency + tests |
| AI payment auth | Hard block + tests |
| Key leakage | Secrets scan + rotation |

## Acceptance

Only CRO (+ CISO for security, Finance for money) may accept residual High/Critical risk — written, time-boxed.

## Reporting

Weekly ops · Monthly platform · Quarterly risk review to Executive Steering.
