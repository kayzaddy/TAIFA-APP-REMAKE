## Standards

- Named service owners (see Ownership)  
- Postmortems blameless, with action items tracked  
- Change management for ledger/identity/prod config  
- Risk register reviewed quarterly with ARB  
- Regional SLOs via continental ops center  

## Problem management

Recurring incidents → problem record → ADR or debt item with owner.
