# Privacy & Compliance Governance

Privacy by design. Continental residency and compliance profiles are the configuration plane — not hardcoded product branches.

## Requirements

- Consent where required for marketing/analytics beyond operational necessity  
- Privacy Impact Assessment for new PII processing  
- Data minimization & purpose limitation  
- Residency / sovereignty via `DataResidencyPolicy`  
- Retention & deletion aligned with audit needs (ledger immutability wins over casual delete)  
- Audit trails for access to restricted data
