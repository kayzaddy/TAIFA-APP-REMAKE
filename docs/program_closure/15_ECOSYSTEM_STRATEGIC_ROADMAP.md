# 15. Taifa Ecosystem Strategic Roadmap (3–5 Years)

**Horizon:** 2026–2031  
**Baseline:** `TAIFA-BASELINE-2026-07-19`  
**Principle:** Expand vertically only after core platforms prove operational and commercial value.

---

## Years 1–2 (Execution & proof)

| Theme | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Core rails | Payments + Ledger production-certified where gates pass |
| Pilots → cert | Winga Hotels + Commerce merchants achieve G6/G7 where exit met |
| Controlled launch | Named cities / merchant cohorts — not national blanket |
| Ops muscle | Incident, DR, support maturity at scale for pilots |
| Integrations | Operator credentials → Integration Certification path |

## Years 2–3 (Controlled expansion)

| Theme | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Geographic | Multi-city after capacity + certification |
| Vertical depth | Winga categories beyond hotels; Commerce MOS depth |
| Liquidity | Marketplace liquidity KPIs green before marketing scale |
| Shared services | Analytics + AI assist scale under governance |

## Years 3–5 (Ecosystem products)

| Future platform | Entry condition |
| --- | --- |
| Government · Health · Education · Agriculture · Tourism · Housing · Employment · Insurance | Inherit lifecycle Stages 0–9; pass G0–G8; reuse Identity/Payments/Ledger/Wallet/Notifications/Audit; ARB approval; no parallel money rails |

## Strategic non-goals

- Designing many platforms before operating few well  
- National claims without evidence  
- Architecture churn as a growth strategy  

## North-star measures (not features)

Availability · Security posture · Ops maturity · Deployment frequency · Incident rate · MTTR · Adoption · GMV · Revenue · Merchant/provider growth · Liquidity · CSAT · Certification progress · Trust.

**End state:** Taifa as a trusted national digital ecosystem — operated, certified, and governed — not a catalog of unfinished products.
